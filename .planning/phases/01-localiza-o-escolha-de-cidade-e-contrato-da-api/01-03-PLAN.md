<objective>
Layer the location capability onto the walking skeleton: a priming screen that requests location
only on an explicit tap, GPS + reverse geocoding to detect and enter the visitor's served city,
and every location outcome modeled as an explicit sealed state with its own UI — never a generic
error. This is the vertical slice that turns "pick a city from a list" into "the app finds your
city for you, and every fallback has a graceful home."

Purpose: satisfy LOC-01, LOC-02, LOC-03, LOC-06 — the heart of APP01. Reuses the tracer's
repository/persistence and expands its minimal screen.
Output: gateways over geolocator/geocoding, the DetectarCidadeUseCase, the 7-variant sealed
`CidadeSelecaoState`, the `CidadeSelecaoCubit`, the priming screen, and the exhaustive
city-selection screen.
</objective>

<user_story>
**As a** visitante na primeira abertura, **I want to** deixar o app descobrir minha cidade pela
localização (ou escolher manualmente se eu recusar), **so that** eu entro direto na vitrine da
minha cidade sem esbarrar numa tela de erro em nenhum desfecho.
</user_story>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| device OS location → app | GPS coordinates and reverse-geocoded placemarks enter the app via geolocator/geocoding. Coordinates are sensitive personal data. |
| app → (no server) | Phase 01 sends nothing to any server; coordinates must stay on-device and out of logs. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-03-01 | Information Disclosure | GPS coordinates / placemark handling in gateways + use case | high | mitigate | Coordinates are transient — used only to resolve the city then discarded; only the resolved city nome+uf is persisted. Never log raw lat/long and never send them anywhere (no network call exists this phase). ASVS V8. |
| T-01-03-02 | (privacy / least privilege) | location permission scope | high | mitigate | Request `whileInUse` only; no `always`/background permission in manifest/plist (Plan 01-01) or in the gateway calls. App-store-review and privacy least-privilege (RESEARCH Anti-Patterns). |
| T-01-03-03 | Denial of Service | geocoding on an unsupported platform / offline | low | mitigate | MissingPluginException/UnimplementedError and geocode failures are caught in the gateway and routed to falhaGeocodificacao → city list; never a crash or dead end (D-12, A1). |
| T-01-03-04 | Tampering | asset parse of the served-city list | low | mitigate | Per-row `fromJson` guard drops malformed rows; parse failure → erroCarregarCidades defensive state, not a fatal crash (V5). |
</threat_model>
