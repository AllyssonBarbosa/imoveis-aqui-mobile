<objective>
Complete APP01's returning-visitor and city-switching experience on top of the location flow: a
one-tap city switcher at the top of the screen, and launch routing that opens directly in the
saved city (validated it is still served) without re-asking for GPS.

Purpose: satisfy LOC-05 and lock in D-08's "reopen goes straight to the saved city, location only
on first run or explicit request." Hardens the tracer's saved-city launch seam with the
stale-city validation flagged in research (A2 / Pitfall 5).
Output: `ValidarCidadeAtendidaUseCase`, the top city switcher widget, and the hardened launch
routing in `main.dart`.
</objective>

<user_story>
**As a** visitante que já escolheu uma cidade, **I want to** reabrir o app já na minha cidade e
trocar de cidade num toque no topo, **so that** eu não preciso refazer a escolha nem esperar o GPS
toda vez que abro o app.
</user_story>
