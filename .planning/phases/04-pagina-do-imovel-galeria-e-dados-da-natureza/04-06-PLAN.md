<objective>
Expansion 4 (presentation slice of the nature data): render `DetalheImovel.dados` delivered by
04-05. Pure functions turn each `DadosNatureza` variant into icon highlights and a label/value list,
a section shows them below the header and before the description, and the embedded building and
block/development get their own cards. Null fields omit their line, empty cards and sections
disappear, and nothing is calculated in the app.

Purpose: success criteria 2 and 3 on screen (DET-04).
Output: `apresentacao_natureza.dart`, `SecaoDetalhe`/`SecaoDescricao`/`SecaoNatureza`, cards, tests.
</objective>

(com o prédio), do terreno ou do lote (com a quadra e o loteamento), **so that** eu entendo o que
estou comprando ou alugando sem precisar perguntar ao corretor.
