vitrine -> `abrirDetalheImovel` pushes the page -> the prévia renders instantly -> `DetalheImovelCubit`

header. Then make a failed load recoverable with 'Tentar de novo'.

mock) on the thinnest path; every later plan expands from this skeleton.
