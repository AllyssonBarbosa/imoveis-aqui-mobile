<objective>
Expansion 3 (data slice of the nature data, split from its presentation per the plan-checker): the
API's per-type block becomes a sealed `DadosNatureza` on `DetalheImovel`, parsed tolerantly by the
same Model the remote source will use, and the simulated server sends a coherent block — including
the embedded building (apartamento) and block/development (lote) — for every property. The lote
example joins the contract.

Purpose: the data half of DET-04 (criteria 2 and 3) and API-06 lockstep; plan 04-06 renders it.
Output: domain sealed class + block models + mapping, fixture blocks, lote example JSON, tests.
</objective>

<user_story>
**As a** visitante olhando um imóvel, **I want to** que o app receba os dados próprios da casa, do
apartamento (com o prédio), do terreno ou do lote (com a quadra e o loteamento), **so that** a página
possa me mostrar o que estou comprando ou alugando sem eu precisar perguntar ao corretor.
</user_story>
