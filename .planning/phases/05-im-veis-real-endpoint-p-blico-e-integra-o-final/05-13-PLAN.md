<assumption_delta_decision>
- Primary noun: `Caracteristica` identified by its API `id`; the name is a display detail.
- Decision: promote — `String` names are replaced by `Caracteristica` everywhere característica lives (options here; draft, filter, chips and query in 05-15), not added alongside.
- Rationale: D-13 makes the id the filter key on the wire; keeping names as the identity would reintroduce the comma risk and mismatches after a rename.
- Transition inside the phase: between 05-13 and 05-15 the sheet marks the draft by the option's name; 05-15 removes that bridge. No debt is carried past the phase.
- Invariant test (05-15 Task 2): every característica of the captured catalog, once marked, round-trips to the query as its id and back to the chip as its name.
</assumption_delta_decision>
