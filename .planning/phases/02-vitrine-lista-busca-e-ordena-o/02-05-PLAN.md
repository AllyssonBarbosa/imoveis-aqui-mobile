<objective>
Complete the vitrine with server-side search (VIT-03) and sorting (VIT-04): the simulated server
learns the search semantics (título + bairro, accent/case-insensitive, D-06), the deterministic
"erro" failure trigger (D-15) and the five orderings with nulls-last price/area (D-11, D-12); the
Cubit gains a 400 ms / 2-char debounced `buscar`, `limparBusca` and `ordenarPor`, all resetting
the list per D-13; the screen gains the SearchBar under the city selector (D-07) and the
"Ordenar: <opção>" bottom sheet (D-10) with room reserved for Phase 3's filters.

Purpose: success criteria 2 and 3 of the phase, keeping every decision about WHAT is listed in
the data layer (core value: nothing recalculated on the device).
Output: shared text normalizer, mock search/sort/trigger, Cubit search/sort, SearchBar, sort sheet,
tests (unit, fake_async, widget, e2e).
</objective>

**As a** visitante na vitrine da minha cidade, **I want to** buscar por título ou bairro e ordenar
por preço, área ou mais recentes, **so that** eu encontro rápido o imóvel que procuro, com a busca e
a ordenação resolvidas no servidor como no site.
